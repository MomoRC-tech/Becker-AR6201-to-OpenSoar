## 1. 🏷️ Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Rechecked source | The exact revision or document was inspected; inferences are stated separately. Photos show appearance only. |
| Prior review | A finding kept from an earlier review, not rechecked. |
| Reported | Someone stated it; not independently checked. Owner-reported measurements are identified explicitly. |
| Assumption / proposed | Hypothesis or design, not yet confirmed. |
| Measured | Recorded setup, versions, input and result. Host tests are recorded here; owner-reported physical measurements are identified separately. |

New evidence records the revision or page, the date, what was checked and its label. Other drivers are not independent proof of the protocol.
