# Ten-board integrated PCB engineering review

20 November 2026 · `research/integrated-pcb-jlcpcb` at `94c4727` · [Native KiCad project](../../../electronics/integrated/compact-bridge.kicad_pro) · [PCB01–PCB09](../../design/requirements.md#requirements) · [Supplier requirements §1.1](../../design/requirements.md#pcb-schematic) · [Evidence S170–S188](../sources.md#source-register)

**Hold fabrication.** The ESP32 antenna overhang and host copper meet the intended layout. The proposed rear wall misses Espressif’s clearance recommendation by 10.19 mm. The electrical release gates are hard input collapse and reversal, surge coordination, ADC switch stress during brownout, and the complete instrument fault return. Connector fit and both exposed-pad processes also need disposition before ordering ten.

## 1. Antenna first — high priority for housing fit

U1 uses the 18 × 6.19 mm antenna area in Espressif’s land pattern. In board coordinates it occupies X121–139, Y106.81–113 mm. Its base meets the routed edge at Y113. The carrier occupies X105–155, Y113–168.5. The complete antenna area projects beyond the carrier, as Espressif recommends. The footprint keepout extends 15 mm to either side and beyond the tip (X106–154, Y91.81–113). It excludes tracks, vias, pads, pours and footprints on all four copper layers. [Espressif Fig. 13 and §11.2](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-wroom-32e_esp32-wroom-32ue_datasheet_en.pdf#page=43)

**Mated access, medium priority.** The loose S43 DE-9 plug spans 30.80 mm in width and the S52 backshell spans 31.50 mm. Centering the latter on the J1 lock midpoint at X138.80 puts its left bounding plane at X123.05. J2’s body reaches X121.65, leaving **1.40 mm between projected widths**. The J1 flange and J2 mouth are staggered by 1.35 mm in depth. The loose DE-9 and XT30 family models have no defined mating transforms here. Check actual plug, thumb, driver and cable clearance with both ports occupied. At H1, the nearest edges of C12 and C19’s 1210 bodies are each 4.20 mm from the hole center. A hypothetical Ø5.4 mm screw head would leave only 1.50 mm before a washer, fit tolerance or tool access. Check board flex at these capacitors when tightening the actual fastener. **Recommendation.** Retain the compact envelope for an RF comparison before selecting a housing change. At the same phone positions, distances and orientations, compare the bare board with the closed housing, actual fasteners, mated plugs, restrained harness and proposed daughterboard envelope. Record range, useful throughput, dropped streams and reconnects with three Wi-Fi streams, then Bluetooth active and in fallback. Use a representative installed orientation. A wall or harness change needs its own decision if performance misses the agreed operating range. Check plug insertion, strain relief, screw access, PCB strain and lid clearance on the physical assembly. ## 2. High-priority electrical findings

| Circuit and condition | Evidence and consequence | Proposed action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 / U4, input short or live reversal | Cold reverse blocks at Q1’s body diode. With VIN_PROT already charged, R10 still pulls the gate below the source and the on-channel conducts back toward J2. If VIN falls below VOUT, C14–C17 can discharge through L1 and U4’s SW-to-VIN parasitic diode. The 88 µF nominal output bank alone stores 0.48 mJ at 3.3 V. Peak reverse current depends on impedance and timing. | Establish the credible live-reversal envelope on a current-limited fixture. If abrupt input short is in scope, propose TI’s VOUT-anode/VIN-cathode Schottky individually. Assess any need for reverse-current blocking at Q1 separately. [TI §10](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr33610.pdf#page=27) · [Q1 terminal diagram](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/DMP6023LE.pdf) |
| U4.4 PG / R11 / U5.1, collapsing input | R11 pulls PG to the stored 3.3 V rail. Its 0.33 mA nominal sink is below TI’s 5 mA DC limit. PG must also stay ≤VIN + 0.3 V. TI characterizes low-VIN PG at EN low and 50 µA. Here EN is tied to VIN. PG filtering can delay a state change by up to 170 µs. A false divider selection or PG pin stress is possible. | Capture VIN, VOUT, PG and GPIO34 on abrupt loss and rapid restart. Assess PG protection separately if it exceeds the VIN-relative limit. A larger pull-up alone does not establish compliance. [TI §§7.1, 7.5, 8.3.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr33610.pdf#page=4) |
| U5.3 S1B / V_DIV / GPIO34, surge overlapping brownout | At VIN_PROT = 32.4 V, with PG selecting ground, the disconnected R8/R9 node tends to 3.237 V. At VDD = 1.5 V TI permits 3.0 V. Assuming ±1% resistors, the upper divider corner reaches 3.30 V and crosses 3.0 V at about 29.5 V input. Duration depends on C18 and the real pulse. At VDD = 0, TI specifies powered-off isolation to 3.6 V. At a hypothetical 36 V protected input the same resistor corner also exceeds that limit (3.66 V). Partial rail voltage also matters at the ESP32 input. | Characterize the input envelope with a dummy front end first. On the board, observe U5 VDD, V_DIV, GPIO34 and rail injection within part ratings. For an assumed 36 V ceiling, propose R8 = 127 kΩ, ±1%, retaining R9/R12. Worst-case disconnected voltage becomes 2.95 V at 36 V before leakage. The normal ADC range becomes 0.51–1.46 V for 7–20 V and requires recalibration. Confirm the ceiling and leakage margin before selection. This decision is independent of ADC filtering. [TI §§6.3, 6.5, 8.3.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux1574.pdf#page=5) |
| J2 / Q1 / D1 / D2 / U4, positive surge | Surge current flows from external F1 and Q1 through D2 to J2 minus. The Q1 and D2 positive paths use vias to reach In2.Cu. SMBJ20A is rated 32.4 V at 18.6 A on a 10/1000 µs pulse. This leaves 3.6 V to U4’s 36 V operating ceiling at that test point, before local ringing. U4 VIN absolute range is −0.3 to 38 V. Assuming a 12 V D1 clamp, R10 carries 0.204 mA at 32.4 V. The specified 11.4–12.6 V zener range is at 5 mA. Q1 permits ±20 V gate-to-source. | Record harness source impedance, pulse duration/repetition, temperature and fuse time-current curve. Measure VGS at Q1 and VIN at U4, as well as TVS current and inrush. Compare D2 pulse energy, hot rating and fuse clearing against this envelope before selecting any different clamp. [Protection sources S185](../sources.md#source-register) · [TI §§7, 10](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr33610.pdf) |
| J1.3/.6/.7 to J2.1, broken instrument ground | If Becker supply ground opens, its return can pass through the instrument’s data ground, J1.3, B.Cu and XT30 minus. F1 on the bridge positive lead cannot interrupt it. S180’s 3.11 mm × 1.45 mm plus 27.45 mm × 2.00 mm trace gives 9.8 mΩ, 31 mV and 0.10 W at the 3.2 A design case and 85 °C. At 3 A, a 25 mΩ mated contact alone would add 75 mV and 0.225 W. The actual mate and wires govern the full path. | Use a dummy load and representative harness. Measure four-wire drop and temperature across each connector, the board and the complete return. Include a lost navigation supply return through pin 6 or 7 and an open XT30 minus, when bridge current can return through instrument grounds. ## 5. Verification and release boundary

- **Native design:** KiCad 10.0.8 ERC and schematic/PCB parity each reported zero findings. Board DRC reported one silkscreen warning and 34 unconnected items. No supply or ground pad was among those opens. One joins two intentionally isolated-from-GND J1 shell locks on the same logical pad. Check lock-to-shell continuity on the actual connector and document disposition of this DRC item while maintaining shell isolation from circuit GND. The remaining opens include pending PG, ADC, pump and serial signals.
- **Artwork:** Temporary Gerber and separate PTH/NPTH Excellon outputs were inspected against the native pad, mask and paste definitions. The two Ø3.20 mm mounting holes are NPTH. No production package, quote, hardware measurement or aircraft acceptance was produced.
- **Tool limits:** Happy-kicad schematic, PCB, cross-analysis, EMC, thermal and Gerber screening were cross-checked against native geometry and supplier documents. Automated MPN/datasheet coverage is incomplete. C4 is a flying capacitor, D2 has four actual return vias, and the selected buck switches at 400 kHz. The generic EMC flags for these items do not establish a defect. The ideal divider model excludes MOSFET/TVS dynamics, partial-supply chip behavior, ADC sampling and cable parasitics.
- **Next branch gate:** Complete PCB08 probe access and the PCB09 expansion-interface review before routing the remaining signals. Reserve fixture, daughterboard and restrained-wire space outside the antenna region. On the routed revision repeat ERC/parity, refill/DRC, return-path and artwork checks. Bring each proposed circuit, housing or assembly change to the owner individually. [Open items and acceptance owners](../../project/open-items.md#pcb-study)
