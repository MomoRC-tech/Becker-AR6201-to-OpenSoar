# Compact PCB placement and physical review

20 November 2026 · 48 × 46.5 mm, four layers, top-side assembly · [Native board](../../../electronics/integrated/compact-bridge.kicad_pcb) · [Populated view](../../images/integrated-pcb-3d.png) · [S189–S190](../sources.md#source-register).

**Hold fabrication.** The board area falls from 2,775 to 2,232 mm². The six programming pads previously spanned 21.25 mm between end centers. Their 2 × 3 arrangement now spans 3.35 × 6.70 mm. The DE-9 and XT30 still occupy 47.0 mm across the front, including their courtyards. Both connectors moved 2.10 mm left relative to the proposed housing. Reserve cutout and fixture design until both plugs can be tried together. The 60 × 72 × 25 mm housing outline is a study dimension.

## Layout and manufacturing checks

- U4’s C13 pads are 2.44/2.39 mm from VIN/PGND pad centers. The 1.00 mm SW route to L1 measures 4.27 mm. C10 remains local to BOOT and SW. The output-capacitor sense path runs on B.Cu under the inner GND plane. TI Table 9-3 includes 6.8 µH with four 22 µF outputs. Confirm capacitance after DC bias, inductor saturation and switching waveforms on the first article. [TI reference design and layout](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr33610.pdf)
- U1 C2/C3 supply pads sit 2.32/4.50 mm from U1.2. Scope local 3V3 during RF bursts and startup with the actual selected capacitors. C6.1 to the U2 V+ pad improved from 8.89 to 3.63 mm center-to-center. The flying-capacitor loops and serial return paths can be assessed after routing. The independent schematic trace confirmed U2 receiver assignments, U3 Y/Z and A/B pairs, the U5 ground/divider truth table and EN/GPIO0/UART0 wiring. [Circuit and reference-layout review](2026-11-20-integrated-pcb.md)
- Native KiCad 10.0.8 ERC and schematic/board parity each report zero findings. Filled-board DRC with the saved production rules reports one existing antenna silkscreen warning and 34 opens. Nine routed power/fault nets have no open. The 34 include the logical connection between the two isolated-from-GND shell locks. PCB08 diagnostic coverage and PCB09 expansion pinout, current, connector, fixture and retained harness volume precede signal routing. The 3D view includes all 43 component models. Connector finish and the XT30 family body are illustrative.
- The J1 board-lock finished-hole tolerance still needs agreement. Quote U1’s twelve open exposed-pad holes under nine paste windows and U4’s twelve thermal vias under one paste opening separately. No physical assembly, vibration test, environmental qualification or fabrication order forms part of this review.
