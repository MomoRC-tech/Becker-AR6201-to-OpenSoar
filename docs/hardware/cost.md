<a id="scope"></a>

## 2. 📋 Calculation scope

- RS-232 price covers both boards together.
- Resistors and screws carry no additional cost in this calculation.
- Existing instrument harness is reused; no replacement cost included.
- Fuse and D-Sub backshell/locking hardware are assumed covered by the listed allowances; no separate amount is added.
- Excludes shipping, tools, labour, prototype reprints and the existing radio, FLARM, Zander and Android device.
